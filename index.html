Wordle

Guess the five letter word in six tries.

Green is right place, yellow is right letter.

How to play

Guess the five letter word in six tries. The colours after each guess tell you how close you are.

  1. Type a five letter word with your keyboard, or use the on-screen keys.
  2. Press Enter to submit the guess.
  3. Green means the letter is in the word and in the right place.
  4. Yellow means the letter is in the word but in a different place.
  5. Grey means the letter is not in the word at all.
  6. Use the colours to narrow it down. After six guesses the word is revealed.
